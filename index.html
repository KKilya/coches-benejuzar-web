About Us

A workshop built on precision

Tapicería Náutica y de Automoción (CB Marine Upholstery & Automotive) is an upholstery workshop specialised in marine projects — boats, yachts and jet skis — that also creates custom interiors for cars and motorcycles, with more than 10 years of experience.

Every project is treated with the same attention to detail, whether it's a premium yacht, a family car or a touring motorcycle — combining traditional craftsmanship with advanced measurement technology.

Digital precision, applied by hand

In our workshop we use advanced digital measurement technology to achieve maximum precision in custom upholstery projects — on board and on the road.

The Proliner digital measuring system captures exact dimensions and translates them into precise digital patterns — ensuring a perfect fit before a single cut is made.

We are one of the few workshops in the area equipped with this technology.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

Every project is different, so each job is priced individually according to materials, complexity, size and labour. The quote is free and carries no obligation.

Why send a video?

It lets us see the job, its size and its condition before you travel. The assessment is free and we reply the same working day, telling you how we would approach it and what we need for your quote.

How do I send the video?

Tap "Send a video". WhatsApp opens with a message ready: fill in what you have and what you need, then attach the video from your phone gallery.

What can you upholster?

Boats, yachts and jet skis (seats, cushions, biminis, sprayhoods and covers), car interiors (seats, roof linings and steering wheels) and motorcycle seats.

What materials do you use?

Marine fabrics resistant to UV and salt water, leather, Alcantara and other premium materials, chosen with you for each job.

How do you make sure it fits?

The Proliner digital measuring system captures the exact dimensions and turns them into a digital pattern before a single cut is made.

What languages do you speak?

We can help you in English, Spanish or Russian.

Do you come to the boat?

Yes. We go to your boat, take the measurements and fit the finished work.

Which area do you cover?

We work across the Vega Baja and surroundings: Torrevieja, Orihuela Costa, Guardamar, Santa Pola, Elche, Alicante and Murcia. If you are further away, send a video and we'll tell you whether we can help.
