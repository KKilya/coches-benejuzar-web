Coches Benejúzar x PROLINER

Digital precision, applied by hand

Coches Benejúzar uses advanced digital measurement technology to achieve maximum precision in custom upholstery projects.

The Proliner digital measuring system captures exact dimensions and translates them into precise digital patterns — ensuring a perfect fit before a single cut is made.

Coches Benejúzar is one of the few workshops in the area equipped with this technology.
